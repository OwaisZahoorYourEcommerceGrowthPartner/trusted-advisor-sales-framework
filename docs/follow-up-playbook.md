# Follow-Up Playbook

Follow-up is where many sellers destroy trust.

A follow-up should not exist to remind the buyer that you are waiting.

It should help the buyer think better.

## Bad Follow-Up

> Just checking in.

This creates no value.

## Better Follow-Up

> I was thinking about our conversation and one thing I would review first is whether the issue is demand, structure, or profitability. Each one would require a different plan.

This shows thinking.

## Follow-Up Timing

Use judgment, but a simple rhythm is:

- 2-4 business days after an active conversation
- 1-2 weeks after a soft pause
- 30-60 days after a clear "not now"
- Immediately only when you have something genuinely useful to add

## Follow-Up Types

### 1. Value Follow-Up

Share a specific idea.

> One thing I would look at is whether spend is concentrated in catch-all structures. Those can hide both waste and profitable pockets.

### 2. Context Follow-Up

Connect your note to something the buyer said.

> You mentioned profitability was the bigger concern than traffic. That changes how I would think about the account.

### 3. Timing Follow-Up

Respect the pause.

> No rush from my side. I know timing matters here. If it becomes useful to revisit this, I am happy to help.

### 4. Strategic Follow-Up

Introduce a bigger business lens.

> If the channel strategy changes, the ad strategy may need to shift from direct ROAS optimization to supporting retail expansion and search visibility.

## The Stay-In-Touch System

When a buyer says "stay in touch," do not disappear and do not chase.

Stay visible through:

- Useful market updates
- Relevant platform changes
- Short observations
- Case-study style lessons
- Thoughtful questions

## The Rule Of One

Every follow-up should have one purpose.

Do not combine:

- A reminder
- A pitch
- A discount
- A new service
- A long explanation

Pick one idea and make it useful.

## Follow-Up After "Not Now"

Template:

> Totally understand. I appreciate the transparency. If timing changes or you ever want a second perspective, I would be happy to help. Wishing you and the team success with the next phase.

Then stop.

Give the buyer room to come back.

## Follow-Up After An Audit

Template:

> Hope everything is going well. Did anything in the audit stand out as worth discussing further?

This works because it asks about the value of the work, not the status of the deal.

## The Best Follow-Up Energy

Calm. Useful. Brief.

Never anxious.

Never entitled.

Never manipulative.
