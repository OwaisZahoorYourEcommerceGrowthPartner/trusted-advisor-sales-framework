# Email Templates

These templates are written for consultative selling. Adapt the language to your voice and the buyer's context.

## 1. Warm Re-Engagement

Subject: Quick question

```text
Hi [Name],

Hope everything is going well.

I was thinking about [channel/account/problem] and wanted to ask how things are performing now.

If there is ever a point where you want a second perspective, I would be happy to take a look and share a few ideas.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## 2. Audit Offer

Subject: [Channel] Audit

```text
Hi [Name],

Thanks for the context.

If helpful, I can review the account and put together a short audit covering where performance may be leaking, what looks under-leveraged, and what I would prioritize first.

No pressure and no commitment. At minimum, you will have a fresh perspective and a few actionable ideas.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## 3. When They Ask For Pricing

Subject: Re: Pricing

```text
Hi [Name],

For this type of work, I usually structure it as a monthly retainer depending on account complexity, spend, and the level of involvement needed.

Before giving a final number, I would prefer to review the current setup so the scope matches what the account actually needs.

If the opportunity is mainly around strategy, optimization, and growth planning, I would expect the engagement to be in the range of [range].

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## 4. When They Challenge Your Plan

Subject: Re: Growth Plan

```text
Hi [Name],

Fair question.

I would not want another shot at the account unless I believed there was a clear opportunity to improve it.

Before making specific recommendations, I would want to review the current data, but my process would focus on three areas:

1. Profitability — identifying wasted spend, inefficient structure, and budget allocation issues.
2. Scale — finding where we can increase visibility without sacrificing economics.
3. Positioning — understanding which products, search intents, and competitors create the best growth path.

The goal would not be to simply manage activity. The goal would be to build a more profitable and scalable growth engine.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## 5. After Sending An Audit

Subject: Audit & Recommendations

```text
Hi [Name],

Thanks again for giving me access to review the account.

I put together an audit covering the current structure, budget allocation, profitability considerations, growth opportunities, and the areas I would prioritize first.

I approached this from a business perspective rather than only looking at campaign-level metrics:

- Where budget is creating the strongest return
- Where spend may be leaking
- Which search intents and products deserve more investment
- What could improve profitability without simply reducing scale
- How I would approach the next 90 days

I have attached the audit for your review.

If helpful, we can also schedule a call and walk through the findings together.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## 6. When They Say Not Now

Subject: Re: [Topic]

```text
Hi [Name],

That makes complete sense. I appreciate the transparency.

I understand not wanting to make changes while the current strategy is still in motion.

I am glad the review surfaced a few useful callouts. If things change down the road or you ever want another perspective, I would be happy to help.

Wishing you and the team success with the next phase.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## 7. Long-Term Relationship Touchpoint

Subject: Thought this might be relevant

```text
Hi [Name],

Hope you are doing well.

I came across something that reminded me of our previous conversation around [topic].

The key takeaway is [one useful insight].

Not sure if it is relevant to your current strategy, but I thought it was worth sharing.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## Template Rule

Every email should feel like it could be forwarded internally without embarrassing you.

That is the standard.
