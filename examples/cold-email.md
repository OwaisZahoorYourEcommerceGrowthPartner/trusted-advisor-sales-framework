# Cold Email Example

Cold outreach should not feel like a pitch deck in email form.

It should feel like a useful observation.

## Email

```text
Hi [Name],

I noticed [specific business observation].

One thing I would be curious to understand is whether [specific issue] is limiting [business outcome].

I work with brands on [area of expertise], mostly around identifying where growth is being limited and where spend or operational effort can be better allocated.

If useful, I would be happy to share a few thoughts.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## Example

```text
Hi [Name],

I noticed your brand has strong product positioning but appears to be underrepresented across a few high-intent marketplace searches.

One thing I would be curious to understand is whether the issue is visibility, conversion, or how the account is allocating spend across product lines.

I work with brands on marketplace growth, advertising structure, and profitability-focused scaling.

If useful, I would be happy to share a few thoughts.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## Why It Works

- Specific enough to feel researched
- Low pressure
- Focused on business diagnosis
- Does not ask for a meeting too early
