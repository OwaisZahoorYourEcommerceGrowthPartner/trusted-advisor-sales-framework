# Turning "No" Into "Not Now"

This example is for a buyer who is not ready to make changes but wants to keep the relationship open.

## Buyer Reply

```text
We are not making changes to the current setup right now, but let's stay in touch. Things can change quickly.
```

## Bad Response

```text
Are you sure? I can do better than your current person and I can also offer a discount.
```

## Better Response

```text
That makes complete sense. I appreciate the transparency.

I understand not wanting to make changes while the current strategy is still in motion.

I am glad the review surfaced a few useful callouts. If things change down the road or you ever want another perspective, I would be happy to help.

Wishing you and the team success with the next phase.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## Why It Works

- Respects timing
- Avoids desperation
- Keeps the relationship warm
- Reinforces professionalism
- Makes it easy for the buyer to return later
