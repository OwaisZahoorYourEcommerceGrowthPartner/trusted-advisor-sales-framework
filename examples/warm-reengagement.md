# Warm Re-Engagement Example

Use this when you already know the buyer and want to reopen a conversation without sounding desperate.

## Email

```text
Hi [Name],

Hope everything is going well.

I was thinking about [channel/account] recently and wanted to ask how things are performing now.

If there is ever a point where you want a second perspective, I would be happy to take a look and share a few ideas.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## Why It Works

- Low pressure
- Relationship-based
- Does not assume a problem
- Opens the door for a business conversation

## When To Use It

- Former client
- Past employer
- Warm founder relationship
- Previous project contact
- Dormant lead
