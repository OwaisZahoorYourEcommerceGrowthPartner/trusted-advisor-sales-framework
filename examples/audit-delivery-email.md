# Audit Delivery Email

Use this when sending a strategic audit to a buyer.

## Email

```text
Hi [Name],

Thanks again for giving me access to review the account.

I spent time going through the data from a few different angles and put together an audit covering current structure, budget allocation, profitability considerations, growth opportunities, and the areas I would prioritize first.

A few things stood out that I think are worth discussing — not because they are necessarily "wrong," but because there may be opportunities to improve how advertising dollars are being allocated and, ultimately, improve profitability while continuing to grow the business.

Rather than focusing only on ROAS or individual campaign performance, I approached this from a business perspective:

- Where budget is creating the strongest return
- Where capital may be deployed inefficiently
- Which campaigns and search intents deserve more investment
- Where spend appears to be leaking
- Areas that could improve profitability without simply reducing scale
- A phased roadmap for the next 90 days

I have attached the audit for your review.

If helpful, we can also schedule a call and walk through the findings together. I can explain why I reached each conclusion, answer questions, and discuss what I would prioritize first.

Whether we end up working together or not, I hope the analysis provides value and gives you a few actionable ideas for the business.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## Why It Works

- It frames the audit as useful regardless of whether the deal closes.
- It avoids attacking the current team.
- It positions the sender as a strategic operator.
- It naturally opens the door to a meeting.
